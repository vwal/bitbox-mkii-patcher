FOURTH CUSTOM RELEASE · 2.3.4+c4

New in +c4

PAD → GEN · TOOLS → MSTR → GEN RUN

A clocked pattern for each pad

Each of the 16 pads can play its own rhythm or melody from the internal or MIDI clock. Choose Euclid, Breakcut, Call+Resp, Evolve, Drummap, Random, Acid, Turing or Learn. Set steps, hits, rotation, probability and velocity per pad.

For a first beat, select a kick, press INFO twice, choose Gen → Euclid and turn on Tools → Mstr → Section → GEN Run → Run. The defaults give four kicks per bar. On Granular pads, find the GEN controls at the end of Cnf.

PADS → STATUS STRIP

See the pattern as it plays

Select a running generator. Tall white marks show programmed hits, short gray marks show rests, and a yellow playhead stays on the current step. In the row below, a cyan dash marks each enabled generator and flashes white when that pad sends a note.

The selected pad has a yellow underline. Selecting a pad with its generator off clears the strip. Probability and step conditions can skip a programmed hit.

SAMPLE → LFO/SPD → SPEED

Use Pitch alongside Sample Speed

One forward Sample voice can change duration at 50–200% while every pad can use native Pitch, including the stretched voice. The full Speed range also works at high positive Pitch.

The first eligible voice to request Speed gets the slot. Other voices play at their own Pitch and 100% Speed. SPDBUSY changes to RETRY once the slot is free. Restart the waiting voice or return its Speed to 100% and adjust again. Quick Pitch or Speed edits can make a brief artifact. If crackling persists with several pitched voices and effects, try stopping a voice or bypassing an effect.

SLICER → POS → SYNC ON → SLICE FIT

Fit slices to the beat with Tape

Cut plays at the original rate. Tape changes the playback rate so the whole slice fits its scheduled duration. A 125 ms slice fitted to 250 ms sounds an octave lower. Choose Sync Grid to round the target duration to a musical division.

A new slice launch picks up changes. Tape uses a separate playback path, so a Sample on another pad can still use Speed.

PAD PARAMETER → SELECT → INFO → SOURCE

Four per-pad modulation sources

ENV follows the pad’s ADSR envelope. RAND chooses a new value on each note. CHAOS gives continuously evolving bipolar motion, and NET produces slower movement from four coupled nodes.

Try RAND on pan or a reverb send and retrigger the pad. On Slice Seq, ENV and RAND step the sequence per note, while CHAOS and NET step it as their signals cross zero going upward.

PADS → HOLD A TRIGGER PAD

Stop a long one-shot by touch

Touch a Trigger pad on the 4×4 grid to play or retrigger it as usual. Keep the same finger on it for three seconds to release all voices on that pad. Other pads keep playing and effect tails can ring out.

A fresh pad tap, GEN hit, MIDI or CV can trigger the sound again.

FX1 → INFO → MORE

Shape delay movement and sound

Set modulation rate (0.05–10 Hz), depth (0–10 ms) and stereo phase (0–180°). Drive and Tone shape the repeats. Return dB trims the wet return from −12 to +12 dB.

Codec replaces Drive and Tone with Loss (0–30%, dropping 10 ms frames) and Bandwidth (4–16 kHz). Each preset stores its FX1 settings.

TOOLS → MSTR → SECTION

Modulate Sweep and blend master distortion

In Master FX, set Sweep anywhere between low-pass and high-pass. Open Sweep Mod to move it with EXT1–8 or a clocked triangle LFO from 1/16 note to eight bars. Signed Depth sets the amount and direction.

In Dist Ctrl, Tone shapes the distorted signal, Dist Mix blends it with the clean signal, and Level dB trims the result (−24 to +12 dB). The blend feeds Sweep and the optional compressor on Out1/2.

THIRD CUSTOM RELEASE · 2.3.4+c3

Introduced in +c3

FX1 → TYPE / MORE

Tape, BBD and Codec delay

Choose Digital, Tape, BBD or Codec. Tape adds saturation, frequency loss, wow and flutter; BBD makes dark, drifting repeats; Codec adds accumulating 16 kHz, 4-bit ADPCM character.

FX1 → More adds 0–100% Flutter on every delay type. Zero preserves each type’s base character, including Tape’s built-in flutter.

FX2 / FX3 → MORE

Deeper reverb controls

Room, Hall and Chamber gain Bass x, Xover, Mod Rate, Mod Dpth, Early, Hi Cut and Lo Cut on a second page. Main returns to the original controls without restarting the tail.

The extra controls work independently on FX2 and FX3. Plate retains its original controls.

PADS → TAP SELECTED FX2 AGAIN

FX3: an independent second reverb

Tap the selected FX2 cell again to switch it to FX3, then press INFO. Choose Plate, Room, Hall or Chamber, with its own settings and tail. Tap the selected cell again to return to FX2.

Any pad can feed FX3 from Pos → FX3 Send. Its separate FX3 Out setting selects Out1/2, the stereo FX1/2 pair, or either mono FX jack.

PAD → POS → SEND → INFO

Modulate all three sends

Select FX1 Send, FX2 Send or FX3 Send and press INFO for LFO, CV, velocity, KEY or other native sources. Three slots per send share the stock total of twelve saved modulation routes per pad.

Send modulation acts on the pad’s mixed audio. Voice-dependent sources use one active voice’s value. Recording preserves send levels and routes, and input monitoring uses them while recording stays dry.

MODULATION SOURCE → KEY

KEY tracking

Note position becomes a modulation source: for example, higher notes can open a filter or increase a send. KEY is centered on MIDI note 60 and is separate from the existing PTCH controller source.

Existing source selections and saved routes keep their meanings; KEY is appended after CC.

TOOLS → REC → CV IN

CV scaling and pitch quantizing

Set shared CV Scale from 0–400% and Offset from −100–100%, with immediate updates to held CV. Quantize CV pitch to one of eleven scales with a selectable C–B root.

Defaults are 100%, 0%, Off and C. Scale/Offset apply to all eight EXT modulation sources; quantizing separately affects the native CV-pitch source. Raw gate detection, signal wake, MIDI notes and audio-input modulation stay unchanged.

SLICER → POS → SYNC GRID

Slice duration grid

With Slicer Sync On, choose Free, 1 bar, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32. Slice durations round to whole grid units, with at least one unit.

The grid sets duration. In +c4, Slice Fit lets you choose Cut or Tape for playback. Free preserves native timing and Sync Off bypasses the grid.

PAD → TONE → FIDELITY

Phone, ADPCM and µ-law

Three more manual Fidelity choices: Phone for 8 kHz telephone-style µ-law color, ADPCM for adaptive 4-bit color at 22.05 kHz, and u-Law at 16 kHz.

Available on Sample, Clip, Slicer and Granular pads. Earlier Fidelity settings retain their saved values.

TOOLS → REC → FX OUT

FX returns where you need them

Send delay and reverb returns to Out1/2 or the physical FX1/2 pair, together or independently. Dly1 Rvb2 splits delay to mono FX1 and reverb to mono FX2.

FX3 has its own output selector. Dry pad assignments remain separate from return routing.

TOOLS → MAIN / MSTR → COMPRESSOR

Compress either output pair

Choose Off, Out1/2, FX1/2 or Both. Threshold, Ratio, Attack, Release, Gain and parallel Mix are shared; the two stereo pairs have independent compressor histories.

The Main and Mstr controls edit the same setting. Master distortion and Sweep still process Out1/2.

CLIP → POS → LIVE FLIP / FILL EVERY

Breakcut and periodic fills

Breakcut adds changing sixteenth-note cuts and repeats. Fill every chooses Always or the final bar of each 2-, 4-, 8- or 16-bar phrase, then normal playback resumes.

Periodic timing works with all Live Flip modes on quantized, synchronized Clips. The underlying musical clock keeps its phase.

PITCH / SPEED · TOP-LEFT COUNTER

Live retry and counter reset

A waiting Sample voice stays at 100% Speed until you restart it or return Speed to 100% and adjust again after RETRY. An active Speed modulation route holds the slot through 100% crossings.

Double-tap the bar:beat counter to renumber the next bar as 1:1 while playback and clock phase continue.

TOOLS → DISP → SAVER GFX

A quieter-looking screen saver

Optionally show ten edge-to-edge lines in shades of dark blue, purple and white on black. One line changes every half-second. Off keeps the black-screen saver.

The normal inactivity timeout and wake rules still apply. Both the screen saver and its graphic are disabled by default.

TOOLS → MAIN → RESET

Reset Tools or FX settings

RESET opens a confirmation page with separate Tools and FX choices. Tools restores global settings and saves them immediately. FX restores FX1/FX2/FX3 settings; save the preset to retain that reset.

Both preserve pad assignments, pad parameters and modulation. Memory consolidation also leaves room for these additions without increasing the internal state reservation.

SECOND CUSTOM RELEASE · 2.3.4+c2

Introduced in +c2

PAD PARAMETERS → TONE

Fidelity, drive and filters

Shape Sample, Clip, Slice and Granular pads. Fidelity extends Interp with SP1200, SP-12, S950, MPC60 and SP Raw colors, plus 12-bit crunch at 16, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4 or 2 kHz. Normal and HighQ are still available.

Add 0–100% pre-filter Drive. Slope 1 sets the primary filter to 12 or 24 dB/octave. Filter 2 adds an independent 24 dB low-pass or high-pass with its own Res 2. Select Drive, Filter 2 or Res 2 and press INFO for LFO/CV modulation.

CLIP → POS

Loop timing and beat repeats

Soften a seam with 0–50 ms Loop Fade, shift the source by −100 to +100 ms, and choose Full or ¼–32 bars of Play Len. Sync adds a 1/32 division.

Live Flip offers Beat Rpt (1–2–2–2), Half Rpt (1–2–1–2), Rotate (2–3–4–1), and eighth- or sixteenth-note fills on beat four. Switch Off to rejoin the current beat. Play Len and Live Flip appear with Quant Size enabled and a timed Sync division.

FX → REVERB · PAD RECORDING

Reverb and recording sends

Choose Room, Hall or Chamber alongside the original Plate. The three new types offer Size, Diffusion and Width. All four have Level.

FX1 and FX2 sends survive recording. Hear the live input through the selected pad’s sends while recording a dry WAV.

TOOLS → COMP / MSTR

Compress, distort and sweep the mix

Set compressor threshold, ratio, gain and parallel Mix under Comp. Under Mstr, choose Comp env for attack and release. +c3 expands Compressor from on/off to an output-bus selector.

Choose Master FX for Soft, Hard, Fold or Tube distortion with up to +24 dB Drive. Turn Sweep left for low-pass or right for high-pass. On Out 1/2, distortion and sweep process the mix and FX returns before the optional compressor.

TOOLS → MIDI → MONITOR

Inspect incoming MIDI

Read notes, CCs, program changes, pressure, pitch bend and transport in a timestamped 32-event history. Freeze, scroll, filter by channel, clear or resume. Clock and Active Sensing have separate activity lights.

PAD PARAMETERS

Pad and navigation fixes

Slice Mono now honors its saved setting after converting a polyphonic Sample pad. On a blank pad, INFO twice opens Sample parameters with LFO/Spd and Speed at 100%.

The configuration tab reads Cnf to make room for Tone. Returning from its modulation editor restores the selected Tone dial and encoder bank.

FIRST CUSTOM RELEASE · 2.3.4+c (c1)

Introduced in +c(1)

Waveform amplitude zoom

Pinch or spread vertically from 25–3200%. Horizontal zoom controls time. A two-finger tap restores 100% amplitude and fits the waveform horizontally.

Pitch-preserving Sample Speed

Set 50–200% under LFO/Spd and assign LFO or CV with INFO. In +c4, one eligible Sample voice can use Speed while native Pitch works on every pad, including that voice.

Screen saver and signal wake

Under Tools → Disp, choose disabled (default) or 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. The first physical input wakes only. Optional MIDI, CV-trigger or combined signal wake lets the first musical event through. MIDI clock and active sensing are excluded from wake activity.

Double-tap dial defaults

Double-tap a numeric dial to restore its base value and keep its modulation assignments. Tools shortens MIDI In to MIDI and shows the full custom version at the top right.

AFTER DOWNLOADING

Install on your Bitbox MkII

See your Bitbox MkII manual for the full flashing procedure. Back up your card before updating.

The exact name matters. The patched file on the SD card must be named BITBOXMK2.bin. If your Downloads folder already contains that name, your browser may add (1), 1 or another suffix. Rename the patched download before copying it.

  1. Copy the patched file. Place BITBOXMK2.bin in the SD card’s root directory, outside any folder.
  2. Insert the card with the unit powered off. Hold INFO while powering on, then release INFO when the upgrade message appears.
  3. Wait for the automatic reboot. Allow about 30 seconds and leave power on until the update finishes.
  4. Confirm 2.3.4+c4. Press HOME until the Menu screen appears. The version is in its lower-right corner, and also at the top-right of Tools.

RETURN TO STOCK

Return to official firmware

To remove the customization, reinstall the original firmware using the same update procedure. Back up your card before reflashing.

Download and extract official Bitbox MkII 2.3.4. Use its original, unmodified file directly.

  1. Replace the patched file on the SD card. Copy the original firmware to the card’s root directory, replacing the patched file. Its name must be exactly BITBOXMK2.bin.
  2. Power off and insert the card. Hold INFO while powering back on, then release INFO when the upgrade message appears.
  3. Wait for the automatic reboot. Allow about 30 seconds and leave power on until it finishes. Confirm 2.3.4 in the lower-right corner of the Menu screen.
