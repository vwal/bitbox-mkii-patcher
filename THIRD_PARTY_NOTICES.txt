Tone T1–T7 use adaptations of Portkit's filter.c, including its sampler-colour
coefficients and state-variable filtering. Upstream snapshot:
https://github.com/ddk006/portkit-micro
tree 3e691535ccf41c8fd33cb15f044ec3045b20b0a4

T4 also adapts Portkit reverb.c for Room/Hall/Chamber, the seam-fade approach
from clipshift.c, and the native-compressor control approach from master.c.
The MkII hooks, bounded tail reset, preset integration, and parallel graph
blend are adapted for this firmware. Reverb Room derives from blackbox-mod.

T5 additionally adapts Clip Shift/Play Len and Sync 1/32 from clipshift.c,
and recording send preservation/monitor routing from recmon.c. The MkII
implementation uses sample-rate-correct rotation, paired read/prefetch hooks,
and extracts/routes the monitor contribution before processing child graph
nodes. Native MkII preset storage and Slice Mono replay handling are integrated
separately.

T6 adapts the master.c distortion shapers, oversampling FIR and gain conversion
from the same Portkit snapshot. The master bus hook, sweep, UI and Clip pattern
mapping target the MkII.

stmlib state-variable filter. The applicable MIT notices follow. These licenses
