Release 2.3.4+c4 includes the G4 adaptations identified below. The GEN-DONOR-LICENSE.txt and GEN-APACHE-2.0.txt notices accompany this release. The stock 1010music firmware is supplied separately by 1010music.

The fourth release draws on the delay modulation, Codec loss, master parallel distortion and synced Slicer Tape work in semierendonmez-commits/bitbox-mk2-semi at b7dfa9ab815342914ade82585756bd701b0c99d7 (MIT). The delay and master DSP build on the Portkit effects credited below. G4 uses local Slicer fragment bounds and read handling, preset integration, status-strip rendering and FX control pages. See GEN-DONOR-LICENSE.txt for the donor license.

G3 adapts the ENV, RAND, Lorenz CHAOS and coupled-node NET equations from semierendonmez-commits/bitbox-mk2-semi at b7dfa9ab815342914ade82585756bd701b0c99d7 under the MIT license. The GEN status strip was inspired by that revision's overlay. G3's fixed per-pad source storage, note-edge detection, queued routing, preset codec additions, native-canvas drawing, and verified keyed GEN event dispatch are local integrations. See GEN-DONOR-LICENSE.txt and the GEN attribution below.

Release 2.3.4+c3 includes adaptations credited below. Thanks to semierendonmez-commits (https://github.com/semierendonmez-commits) and bitbox-mk2-semi, the portkit contributors, Justin Joe's blackbox-mod for Fidelity/filter work, and Emilie Gillet's stmlib for the state-variable filter. The +c3 release used a local Breakcut/fill mapping. The fourth release adds the separate donor-derived GEN sequencer credited below. Airwindows processors, themes and the donor's BL encoder were outside these imports. The 1010music firmware is not included in the public patch distribution and is not covered by these licenses. Share the patcher and build instructions, not patched firmware images.

M6 adapts KEY-source exposure, Slice Sync Grid and shared CV scale/root ideas from bitbox-mk2-semi (MIT, portkit contributors and semierendonmez-commits), pinned commit b7dfa9ab815342914ade82585756bd701b0c99d7. Send modulation uses this project's queued source binding rather than the donor's temporary pad-copy approach. The CV subpage, raw-gate preservation, held-CV refresh, send recording/monitor integration and bounded state layout are local integrations. The existing Portkit MIT notice below applies to adaptations.

M5: FX3 send hooks, grid-cell switching and the 48/29.8 kHz reverb adapter are adapted from bitbox-mk2-semi (MIT, pinned commit 864ce16b170f83743c766bdf75bb28e393d658e5), itself based on portkit-micro. This integration uses independently reserved buffers, separate reverb contexts, local FX routing and existing native preset records. Original notices follow.

M4 adapts the adjustable flutter depth and DMA2D palette-reload approach
from bitbox-mk2-semi at the pinned snapshot below. The saver colours,
cache maintenance, counter renumbering, periodic clip mapping, native dual-bus
compressor integration and live neutral/retry changes are local implementations.

M3 additionally adapts the Codec-delay generation-loss algorithm and MkII FX
return bus map from the same bitbox-mk2-semi snapshot credited below. Its
mono split mixer and native-layer line screen saver are local implementations.

M2 adapts the G.711 / IMA ADPCM round trips and Tape/BBD delay colour from
bitbox-mk2-semi (MIT, portkit contributors and semierendonmez-commits).
Upstream source: https://github.com/semierendonmez-commits/bitbox-mk2-semi
Commit: 864ce16b170f83743c766bdf75bb28e393d658e5.
The existing Portkit MIT notice below applies to these adaptations.

Tone T1–T7 use adaptations of Portkit's filter.c, including its sampler-colour
coefficients and state-variable filtering. Upstream snapshot:
https://github.com/ddk006/portkit-micro
tree 3e691535ccf41c8fd33cb15f044ec3045b20b0a4

T4 also adapts Portkit reverb.c for Room/Hall/Chamber, the seam-fade approach
from clipshift.c, and the native-compressor control approach from master.c.
The MkII hooks, bounded tail reset, preset integration, and parallel graph
blend are adapted for this firmware. Reverb Room derives from blackbox-mod.

T5 additionally adapts Clip Shift/Play Len and Sync 1/32 from clipshift.c,
and recording send preservation/monitor routing from recmon.c. The MkII
implementation uses sample-rate-correct rotation, paired read/prefetch hooks,
and extracts/routes the monitor contribution before processing child graph
nodes. Native MkII preset storage and Slice Mono replay handling are integrated
separately.

T6 adapts the master.c distortion shapers, oversampling FIR and gain conversion
from the same Portkit snapshot. The master bus hook, sweep, UI and Clip pattern
mapping target the MkII.

Portkit credits Justin Joe's blackbox-mod for filter.c and Emilie Gillet's
stmlib state-variable filter. The applicable MIT notices follow. These licenses
cover the credited code; they do not license the original 1010music firmware.
